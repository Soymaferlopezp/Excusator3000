Excusator3000 is a humorous, theatrical excuse generator. Submit a social case, answer the tribunal's interrogation, choose the exact situation you need to escape, and receive a ruling that can become progressively more audacious.

## ⚖️ What is Excusator3000?

Sometimes the difficult part of declining a plan is not the reason—it is finding a reason with the correct level of confidence, specificity, and plausible deniability.

Excusator3000 turns that familiar social problem into a fictional legal proceeding. Instead of a generic text generator, it uses a curated comedy engine to examine the case, constrain it to a precise intent, and issue an authorized excuse with a follow-up answer, weak point, repair instruction, discovery risk, and institutional verdict.

The tribunal metaphor gives every interaction a clear comedic voice and makes the result feel like a collectible case file rather than disposable text. The verdict can be copied, escalated, regenerated, or exported as a shareable image.

## 🎯 The Experience

~~~text
Category
  → Interrogation
  → Required scenario intent
  → Deliberation
  → Verdict
  → Generate Variant / Increase Audacity
  → Copy / Share
~~~

The five current categories are:

- Family
- Friends
- Work
- Studies
- Dating

The complete experience is localized in:

- English — default
- Spanish

The case calibration controls credibility, drama, initial audacity, and relationship tone. Interrogation answers then contribute structured risk and relevance signals before the user selects the exact supported scenario.

## 🔥 Audacity System

Every concept family follows the same escalation:

~~~text
PRUDENT → BOLD → NO RETURN
~~~

The premise stays recognizable while the defense becomes increasingly specific, theatrical, and difficult to justify. **Increase Audacity** preserves the current concept and selects its next authored level instead of switching to an unrelated excuse.

## 🧠 Relevance Engine

Candidate selection follows a deliberate order:

1. Active locale
2. Selected category
3. Required scenario intent
4. Current audacity level
5. Interrogation signals and case configuration
6. Candidate scoring
7. Anti-repeat history

Scenario intent is a hard relevance filter, not a scoring hint. A **Friends + cancel after confirming** case cannot return an excuse about splitting the bill, even if other signals would otherwise make that candidate competitive.

Within the eligible pool, the engine considers authored signals, avoided signals, relationship, credibility, drama, comedy style, weighting, and recent-use penalties. It then chooses among the strongest candidates while favoring fresh results.

## 🎭 Comedy Engine

The comedy is authored, structured, and deterministic rather than generated by an external model.

- English and Spanish have independent curated content banks.
- Each concept family contains a complete PRUDENT, BOLD, and NO RETURN progression.
- A shared <code>conceptId</code> connects those three levels.
- Generate Variant keeps the same case, intent, configuration, and audacity while selecting another eligible concept where the bank supports one.
- Anti-repeat history tracks recent excuses, concepts, verdicts, openings, and institutional punchlines.
- The excuse speaks in a human voice; verdicts, stamps, risk labels, evidence, and instructions speak in the Tribunal's voice.

Institutional citations are intentionally unserious. In Spanish, the tribunal may confidently cite: **“Fuente: de los deseos.”**

## 🛡️ Safety by Design

The product allows absurdity and socially questionable comedy without becoming a tool for serious harmful deception. Requests involving serious illness, death, emergencies, medical/legal/financial/immigration deception, fabricated evidence, discriminatory motives, self-harm, crime concealment, impersonation, harassment, or vulnerable-person manipulation are refused or reframed.

The current MVP does not collect an open-ended situation field. Internally, the engine retains bounded context-classification and safety support, but normal user selection is constrained to the approved intent and content banks.

## 🏗️ Architecture

~~~mermaid
flowchart TD
    U[User] --> I[Category + case calibration]
    I --> Q[Interrogation answers]
    Q --> SI[Required scenario intent]
    SI --> CS[CaseState]
    CS --> SG[Safety gate + structured signals]
    SG --> IF[Hard intent filter]
    IF --> SC[Candidate scoring]
    SC --> AR[Anti-repeat selection]
    AR --> V[Verdict]
    V --> C[Copy]
    V --> SH[Public-safe Share Card]
    V --> A[Increase Audacity]
    V --> VR[Generate Variant]
~~~

The MVP runs without an application database, custom backend service, or external LLM API. Content, selection, localization, and verdict assembly live in the TypeScript application. Browser storage is used only for lightweight preferences and recent-result history.

## 🔄 Generation Semantics

| Action | Invariant |
| --- | --- |
| Generate Variant | Same case, configuration, required intent, and audacity; another eligible concept where possible |
| Increase Audacity | Same concept; advance to the next authored audacity level |
| Change language | Preserve the active case and relocalize it; preserve the intent only when valid in the target locale |
| Click the logo | Return to Home and initialize a fresh case without a page reload |

If an intent has no valid counterpart after a language switch, only the intent is cleared and the user must select a supported option before a new verdict can be issued.

## 🖼️ Share Card

## 🧩 Adding or Editing Content

The runtime banks are:

- <code>src/engine/content.en.bank.ts</code>
- <code>src/engine/content.es.bank.ts</code>

Each authored candidate follows the current <code>ExcuseBlock</code> shape:

~~~ts
interface ExcuseBlock {
  id: string;
  conceptId?: string;
  body: string;
  followUp: string;
  weakness: string;
  repair: string;
  signals?: GenerationSignal[];
  avoidSignals?: GenerationSignal[];
  relationships?: Relationship[];
  credibilities?: Credibility[];
  dramas?: Drama[];
  styles?: ComedyStyle[];
  weight?: number;
}
~~~

Content maintenance rules:

1. Give every candidate a deterministic, unique <code>id</code>.
2. Use one stable <code>conceptId</code> across the PRUDENT, BOLD, and NO RETURN versions.
3. Keep every concept family complete at all three audacity levels.
4. Register the concept under a supported scenario intent in <code>scenarioIntents.ts</code>.
5. Treat imported or source-authored <code>body</code>, <code>followUp</code>, <code>weakness</code>, and <code>repair</code> as curated copy—not casual rewrite material.
6. Preserve safety filters, relevance boundaries, variant behavior, and escalation continuity.
7. Validate that every concept is mapped exactly once and that no ID or audacity level is orphaned.

A scenario-intent entry is deliberately small:

~~~ts
{
  id: "friends-cancel-confirmed",
  label: "Cancel after saying you are definitely coming",
  conceptIds: ["amigos-01-saying-you-are-definitely-coming-and-never-showing-up"],
}
~~~

The registry is locale-specific, so English and Spanish may support different option sets without mixing their content.

## 🏆 Hackathon / Why It Matters

People do not always need another productivity tool. Sometimes they need an absurdly over-engineered institution to validate not going somewhere.

Excusator3000 has an immediately understandable premise, a strong tribunal identity, short replayable sessions, escalating authored comedy, and a result designed to be shared. Its curated deterministic engine keeps jokes intentional and localized instead of delegating the product's voice to generic model output.
